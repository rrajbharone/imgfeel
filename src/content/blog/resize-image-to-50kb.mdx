## File Format Selection (JPG vs. PNG vs. WebP for 50KB)

Choosing the right format depends on the specific document type and application:

### 1. JPG / JPEG (Universal Choice for Forms & Passports)
- **Ideal For**: Passport photographs, identity cards, scanned certificates, and job applications.
- **Why It Works**: JPG's discrete cosine transform compression delivers rich gradients, realistic skin tones, and optimal byte economy under 50 KB.

### 2. PNG (Best for Crisp Line Art & Signatures)
- **Ideal For**: Digital signatures, institutional stamps, vector illustrations, and transparent graphics.
- **Why It Works**: Lossless Deflate encoding keeps high-contrast pen strokes perfectly sharp with zero ringing artifacts around letters.

### 3. WebP (Best for Modern Web & App Design)
- **Ideal For**: Blog banners, website hero cards, and mobile app avatars.
- **Why It Works**: Modern predictive encoding offers up to 35% higher visual density than JPEG at identical 50 KB file sizes.
