## Image Format Optimization Guide (JPG, PNG, WebP)

Choosing the right image format ensures optimal results at 40 KB:

### 1. JPG / JPEG (Best for Photographs & Portal Uploads)
- **Ideal For**: Passport headshots, identity cards, portraits, and multi-colored documents.
- **Why It Works**: JPG's lossy compression takes advantage of the human eye's sensitivity to brightness over color, achieving rich photographic detail under 40 KB.

### 2. PNG (Best for Graphics & Line Art)
- **Ideal For**: Flat logos, digital signatures, rubber stamps, and transparent background graphics.
- **Why It Works**: Uses lossless Deflate encoding that preserves razor-sharp edges without compression noise. (Note: Complex photos in PNG format may exceed 40 KB; converting photos to JPG is recommended).

### 3. WebP (Best for Modern Web & Mobile UI)
- **Ideal For**: Web hero banners, blog graphics, user profiles, and e-commerce thumbnails.
- **Why It Works**: Modern predictive encoding delivers up to 35% higher visual quality than JPEG at equivalent 40 KB file weights.
