## File Format Selection Guide (JPG vs. PNG vs. WebP at 60KB)

Selecting the appropriate output format ensures maximum image quality at 60 KB:

### 1. JPG / JPEG (Best for Photos & Colored Documents)
- **Ideal For**: Passport headshots, outdoor photography, multi-colored certificates, and job applications.
- **Why It Works**: JPG's discrete cosine transform compression provides rich color gradients and realistic skin tones under 60 KB.

### 2. PNG (Best for Sharp Line Art & Signatures)
- **Ideal For**: Official stamps, digital signatures, vector icons, and transparent background graphics.
- **Why It Works**: Lossless Deflate encoding keeps high-contrast pen strokes perfectly sharp with zero ringing artifacts around letters.

### 3. WebP (Best for Modern Web & Mobile UI)
- **Ideal For**: Web hero banners, blog graphics, user profiles, and e-commerce thumbnails.
- **Why It Works**: Modern predictive encoding delivers up to 35% higher visual density than JPEG at equivalent 60 KB file weights.
