Selecting the correct output image format is critical depending on your specific use case:

- **Ideal For**: Passport headshots, outdoor photography, multi-colored certificates, and government form uploads.

## Common Errors & Troubleshooting

When submitting images to automated online forms, avoid these frequent rejection pitfalls:

> **The 30.01 KB Trap**: If an application specifies a 30 KB maximum limit, uploading a file that is 30.1 KB or 30,800 bytes will trigger an immediate rejection error. ImgFeel's target size engine automatically targets slightly below the ceiling (e.g. 27 KB – 29.5 KB) to ensure 100% compliance.

### Common Mistakes and How to Fix Them
- **Wrong Aspect Ratio**: Squeezing a rectangular landscape photo into a square box distorts the face. Crop your image to the correct 1:1 or 3.5 × 4.5 ratio before compressing.
- **Blurry Signatures**: Capturing signatures in dim indoor light results in grayish backgrounds. Take signature photos in bright natural light on pure white unruled paper.
- **Incorrect File Extensions**: Ensure the file extension matches the MIME type (`.jpg` for JPEG, `.png` for PNG).
